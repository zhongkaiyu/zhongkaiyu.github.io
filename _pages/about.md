I am currently a second-year PhD student in the CSE department of UC San Diego (since 2024), working with Prof.[Yufei Ding](https://sites.google.com/ucsd.edu/yufeiding/people/prof-yufei-ding). Before coming to UCSD, I obtained my master's degree from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), under the supervision of Prof. [Yunji Chen](https://novel.ict.ac.cn/ychen/) and Prof. [Xing Hu](https://xinghu-cs.github.io/). Prior to that, I received my bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I worked closely with Prof. [Zhuoran Song](https://songzhuoran.github.io/).

My research centers on the co-evolution of LLM and hardware systems: I explore novel architectures and systems to accelerate LLM workloads, while harnessing LLMs to advance chip design. My work lies at the intersection of Computer Architecture, ML Systems, and AI4Chip.

Also, shout-out to my roommate Zaifeng, who is an expert in kernels, systems, and "cooking". Check out his [homepage and research](https://panzaifeng.github.io).
